# Task Rubrics

Task rubrics start from the resolved project quality profile and apply deterministic task-type extensions and risk-sensitive criteria. Overrides that lower thresholds, exclude mandatory criteria, or raise risk require an approval record linked to the review or change request. Reviewers cannot omit an applicable criterion.

The task rubric is evaluated independently of the staged specification gate. A
passing weighted score does not satisfy a missing acceptance criterion; specification
findings and `spec_compliance.status` remain authoritative for Stage 1.
